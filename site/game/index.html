The device is the gamepad. If your Claude Mate is linked and the daemon is running with --web, open this page from http://127.0.0.1:8788 and the four keycaps on the hardware drive the game above — the badge turns green. Everyone else plays on the keyboard: ← → to walk, ↑ to jump, esc for the menu. Sound is synthesised in the browser; nothing is downloaded.
